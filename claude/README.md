# Portal Imobiliário plugin for Claude

Connects Claude to [Portal Imobiliário](https://portalimobiliario.com.pt), the real estate portal for Portugal, through its remote MCP server at `https://portalimobiliario.com.pt/mcp` (OAuth sign-in with a Portal Imobiliário account).

## What you can do

- Search homes for sale and for rent anywhere in Portugal, with filters for area, type, price, size, bedrooms, features, condition and new developments.
- See how each asking price per m² compares with the market value of its municipality (official INE data) and look for listings below market value.
- Check prices per m² for any district, municipality or parish: official sale and rent prices, yearly change, bank valuations and the asking prices on the portal.
- Open a listing with its description, photos, areas, energy class and advertiser.
- Save favourites and create alerts for new listings and price drops.
- Use the property transfer tax (IMT), mortgage and capital gains calculators.
- For agencies and owners: publish and update listings, import files and CRM feeds, manage photos, read enquiries and manage the agency page.

## Skills

- `get-started`: first use, sign-in and what to ask.
- `find-a-home`: turns what someone wants into a search, compares prices with the market and offers an alert.

## Privacy

Searches made through Claude are not linked to the account; the portal keeps only aggregated daily counts of tool use. Advertisers' contact details are never returned. Policy: https://portalimobiliario.com.pt/privacidade
